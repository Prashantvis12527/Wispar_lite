# Insights tab

- **Words per minute:** words / recording minutes. Clips under 2 s or 3 words are ignored (noisy). Also shows how many times faster than typing at 40 wpm.
- **Total words dictated:** all-time count, plus today, this week and about how many pages (500 words each).
- **Streak card:** a 26-week heatmap (`Heatmap` in `Dashboard.swift`), shade by words that day, current streak outlined, plus the longest streak. Hovering a square shows that day's word count. A streak stays alive if you dictated yesterday but not yet today.
- All numbers come from `Stats` in `History.swift`, computed from the history file (see `history.md`).
